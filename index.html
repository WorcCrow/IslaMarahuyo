Mabuhay! Welcome to the Island

Explore a tropical open-world packed with deep-sea diving, open-water fishing, competitive bangka racing, and coastal village living! Use this guide to master the island's features, maximize your Shell earnings, and navigate the deep ocean safely.

Palengke Stilt Market

Deliver market crates in the 1 → 2 → 3 order along the stilt deck. Pays 3 Shells per crate + 12 bonus on completion (up to 31 Shells on a streak).

Bantay Lambat (12 Buoys)

Haul fish traps moored around the coast for named catches (Tilapia, Bangus, Lapu-lapu, Tanigue). Cooldowns are per-player so everyone can harvest together!

Scuba Diving & Air Mechanics

Diving is strictly skill and exploration-based—dive tanks cannot be bought with pay-to-win mechanics!

Lung Coral Upgrades

Base breath is 45s. Search Yungib ng Perlas for 3 hidden Lung Corals to permanently boost max breath by +15s each (up to 90s).

Air Refill Rules

Waterline hopping is disabled. Air only refills after standing on dry land or inside dry cave air pockets for 3 continuous seconds.

Guardian Dugong

If breath drops below 12 seconds, a friendly Dugong rescues you and restores 45% air. Drowning teleports you to Nurse Fely for 5s.

WARNING: Patrolling Sharks! Between 3 to 6 Pating (Sharks) patrol open waters daily. They swim at 30% of player swim speed (outrunnable), but a bite deducts 14s of air and shoves you off course!

Travel, Boats & Isla Ningning

Bangka Rental (Outrigger Boats)

Hire outrigger boats at the Bangkaan pier (Kasama VIP rides free):

  • Maikli (1 min): 15 Shells
  • Katamtaman (3 min): 40 Shells
  • Mahaba (5 min): 70 Shells
  • Long Hire (10 min): 180 Shells

Northern Ridge Zipline

Ride cables connecting Tanaw Ridge → Batong Hilaga → Batong Silangan → Batong Timog for 25 Shells per leg (75 Shells full trip).

Secrets of Isla Ningning

Located ~1,269 studs southeast, Isla Ningning is completely out of swimming reach on air alone. Hire a Bangka for a 35-second sail to access exclusive scenic spots, lookout decks, and 3 interactive photo-pad postcard frame camera spots!

Daily Island Tasks & Cabin Plots

Gawain sa Isla (Daily Tasks)

Complete 4 daily server-side validated tasks (+30 to +60 Shells each). Clearing all 4 awards a +50 Shell Bonus! Tasks rotate globally at UTC midnight.

Claiming Cabin Plots

Claim 1 of 6 stilted bahay kubo cabin plots near the villages to place your name on the signboard. Extra Cabin Plot holders can claim up to 2 plots simultaneously.
