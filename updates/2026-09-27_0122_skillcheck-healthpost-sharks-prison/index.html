What was asked

  1. A way to fight back against sharks that is fair to the player without breaking the shark mechanics.
  2. The Health Post cut from 5 box nurses to 2 humanoid nurse avatars that walk around (counter, supplies, medicine) to look busy. It changed during planning into a dock-and-drag revive: a drowned diver lands on a dock about 20 seconds from the hospital, a nurse (bot or player) walks out and drags them in, and laying them on the scanner revives them. With only 2 bots, a rush queues; player nurses add capacity.
  3. A much bigger prison, "like Bilibid", where detainees can roam and run in a quadrangle.
  4. A modular radial skill check system with at least 3 kinds, reusable for the nurse job now and the market later. The user supplied a reference screenshot of a dark ring with a "Click" pill in the middle.

Follow-ups added along the way: a guidance arrow for player nurses that only appears when they get stuck; player nurses wear a nurse costume; the automatic place backups were dropped (see D:\Claude\CLAUDE.md); every change now gets an update page like this one.

1. Radial skill check system

Three kinds, one look (dark ring, warm lit zone, pill label in the centre, title above, hint below), built from Frames only — no image assets.

KindHow it playsScored on
SweepNeedleA marker circles the ring; press as it crosses the lit arc. Repeats for 3 revolutions.press
HoldFillHold to drive the marker round (a trail fills behind it); let go inside the lit arc. Auto-releases once past the arc.release
ShrinkingRingA bright ring closes onto a lit band whose thickness is the hit window; press as they meet.press
  • Server decides. SkillCheckPhase holds the maths used by both sides. A phase is plain data (id, kind, difficulty, startAt, target, period|duration, tolerance, title, subtitle, action) timed with workspace:GetServerTimeNow(). The client reports when it pressed; ClampClaim only believes that within MAX_LAG = 0.3s of when the server heard it.
  • Difficulty is easy|medium|hard; each kind maps it to its own speed and tolerance (table TUNING in SkillCheckPhase). LEAD = 0.55s before the marker moves. HoldFill allows 6s before the first press.
  • Input: click or tap anywhere on the dial, or Space / E / gamepad A / R2 (bound with ContextActionService and sunk, so Space does not also jump).
  • API — server: local hit, quality = SkillCheckServer.Run(player, kind, {difficulty, title, subtitle, action}) (yields; one check per player; Cancel(player)). Client-only, pays nothing: RadialSkillCheck.Play(kind, config).
  • Used by: Health Post lay-down (HoldFill), shark lunge parry (ShrinkingRing), and a cosmetic "Stay with us" check for divers waiting on the dock (random kind, easy, local only).

2. Health Post: dock-and-drag revive

Flow

  1. BreathService drowns the diver as before and calls ReviveService.Begin(player, onComplete). drowning stays set until the revive completes, so nobody waiting can drown again or lose anything more.
  2. The diver is laid on their back on a free mat on the Pantalan dock (16 mats; past 16 they stack on existing mats) and joins a FIFO queue.
  3. Dispatcher (every 0.4s): if a free player nurse is on duty, a new patient is left for players for PLAYER_FIRST_SECONDS = 12; otherwise the nearest free bot is sent at once.
  4. Bot: walks down at 14, takes them by the shoulders (weld plus non-colliding NurseCarry collision group — the same technique as PoliceUnit's escort), drags them at BOT_DRAG_SPEED = 9 (~20s) to a free scanner bay, and a 4s scan revives them.
  5. Player nurse: presses Carry to the Health Post on any mat that is still waiting (this overrides a bot on its way), drags them (SprintScript: walking 11, sprinting 15), presses Lay them in the scanner at a free bay, and plays a HoldFill check. A hit bills NURSE_FEE = 60; a miss bills 60% (capped at what the patient has). The patient is revived either way. Carries time out after 100s and go back to the front of the queue. Prisoners serving a nurse sentence do the same work for service credit.

3. Shark defense (non-lethal)

  • Lunge + parry. A shark in bite range no longer bites on the spot: startLunge holds it coiled and thrashing just out of reach (holdLunge) while the diver gets a ShrinkingRing check "Shark! Fend it off". Hit: driveOff (knocked back, flees for FLEE_SECONDS = 18, ignores every diver, health reset). Miss: the original bite() (breath cost, ragdoll launch), unchanged. Difficulty is easy with the knife in hand and medium without; night is one step harder. There is one lunge per diver at a time.
  • Dive Knife (StarterPack.DiveKnife, a free tool everyone always has, never lost on drowning). Stabs are validated by the server (RequestSharkHit: knife in hand, reach 10, 0.5s cooldown). SHARK_HEALTH = 3 hits drive a shark off. Each hit shoves it back 3 studs and makes it leave that diver alone for 2.5s. A stab that drives off a shark lunging at someone else cancels that diver's check, and no bite follows.
  • Sharks are never destroyed. The day/night population logic is untouched and there is nothing to farm.
  • Hotbar order: DiveKnife = 2.5 (after the O2 tank) in both InventoryService.HOTBAR_RANK and Hotbar.ORDER.

4. Prison: Piitan ng Marahuyo

  • new Workspace.IslaMarahuyo.Prison, south of the police station. Walls x −196..−84, z −124..−194, ground y 18. Terrain was levelled there (a FillBlock of air above, ground below, a sand wedge down to the beach on the south side).
  • Contents: 14-stud concrete walls with a cap, foot stripe and barbed wire; 4 corner guard towers with searchlights (SpotLight); a closed barred gate on the north wall (x −98..−88) under a "PIITAN NG MARAHUYO" name board; an 11-cell block along the north wall (Cells/Selda_1..11, open doorways, double bunks, bench, number plate, a HoldSpot each) under a tin roof with a walkway; the quadrangle with a basketball court (lines, 2 hoops), a covered mess area (3 tables), wall benches, pull-up bars, and a flagpole with a Philippine flag; a community-service desk (ServiceDesk) under an awning inside the gate.
  • Invisible markers: PrisonBounds (the area detainees are held in), Gate/BookingPoint (where escorts end), Gate/ReleasePoint (outside the gate).
  • PoliceService: each detainee is assigned a cell (cellOf, the least-occupied first) and may roam the whole compound. Leaving PrisonBounds puts them back in their cell. The desk prompt is on Prison.ServiceDesk. release() teleports them to ReleasePoint if they are inside.
  • PoliceUnit: a new M.BOOKING_AT (set by PoliceService to the gate). Cuffed suspects are dragged there instead of to the station door. Officers still spawn from, and return to, the station.
  • Five palms/Palm_5 were moved out of the footprint, and bushes/tufts inside it were removed. The station's old holding cell is left as decoration and is no longer used.

How it was tested (Studio playtests)

  • Skill checks: all 3 kinds scored hits with honestly timed presses (quality 0.87–0.94). A late press claiming perfect timing was rejected. The real key-press path (CAS press and release) reached the server.
  • Health Post: a full bot revive took 15s fetch + 21s drag + 4s scan; the idle loop ran; the costume applied on claiming the role; the call-out and the arrow appeared after the idle timeout.
  • Sharks: a lunge showed the hard check at night without the knife, and a miss bit as before. With the knife equipped: a medium check, and a hit led to "Parried!" and the shark fleeing. Three stabs (the real tool click included) drove a shark off.
  • Prison: booked into Selda 1; standing mid-court was not pulled back; standing outside the wall was put back in the cell; release put them outside the gate; the desk prompt exists.
  • Studio-only test hooks (inert on live servers): ServerStorage.DebugDrown:Fire(player), DebugJail:Fire(player, days), DebugRelease:Fire(player). Call them from the Server datamodel. Note: require() in an execute_luau context gives a separate module instance, so fire these hooks instead of calling module functions.
